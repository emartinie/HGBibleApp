## Editorial choices
Seven separately usable days form one weekly arc, each with focus readings, a consideration question, reflection, practice, and prayer. Readers can use their preferred Bible translation; no extended translation quotations are reproduced.
Messianic connections center on John 1's explicit return to creation. Yeshua is explained as Jesus' Hebrew name. Isaiah's address to Israel is retained. Sabbath is introduced from Genesis while acknowledging differing Christian observance. No hidden-letter claims or speculative typology are treated as established fact.
