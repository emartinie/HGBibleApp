## Measured Prayer Map privacy review — no remediation executed

Both HomeGroupsMap.geojson and data/HomeGroupsMap.geojson contain **2,000 records each**; **1,998 per file** have coordinate strings with more than two decimal digits. Fields observed are Name, description, visibility and Coordinates; no embedded consent/publication-proof field was found. Absence of that field does not prove consent was never obtained.
